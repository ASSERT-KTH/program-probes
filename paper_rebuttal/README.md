# Revision figures and tables (brief P-FIG)

Generated by `scripts/paper_rebuttal_figures.py`, which reuses `run_paper_figures.py` and `paper/style.py`, on a compute node. Paths and filenames mirror the paper repo (`figures/*.tex`, `figures/figures/...`). Every number comes from the rerun: task split (70/15/15 by task, seed 42) and real agent turns.

Conventions:
- "Best layer" means the best layer on **test**, as submitted, among the layers 1, 11, 21, 31 and 40 (1-indexed).
- `_weighted_mean` drops single-class position bins, which `src/probe.py` stores as a 0.5 placeholder. This changes only multi-bin evaluations.

| Artifact | AUC definition |
|---|---|
| `figures/auc_table.tex` (tab:auc_roc) | Pooled over all test tokens with y ≥ 0. One AUC per layer, and the controls at their best layer on test. Equal to E17 `real_testbest`. |
| `figures/transfer_table.tex` (tab:transfer) | Transfer rows are the n_test-weighted mean over 10 relative-position bins. The in-dist reference rows are pooled. |
| `figures/dataset_stats_table.tex` | No AUC. Identical to `paper/dataset_stats_table.tex`. |
| `figures/figures/auc_summary_barplot.pdf` | Pooled. Bars and both control markers are at their best layer on test. |
| `figures/figures/combined/layer_auc_grid.pdf` | Pooled, per layer. The control is the token shuffle. |
| `figures/figures/transfer_barplot.pdf` | In-dist bars are pooled. Transfer bars are the mean over 10 bins. All are at their best layer on test. |
| `figures/figures/<run>/currently_*_lookahead[_pro]_max{15,50}.pdf` | Pooled per k. Per-layer probe lines, oracle persistence, and the k=0 probe (validation-best layer) scored on y_{t+k}. |
| `figures/figures/horizon/horizon_*_yt0.pdf` | Pooled within y_t = 0 tokens (E16, window 15). The k-step probe is at its validation-best layer, with a 95% task-bootstrap CI. |

Not regenerated, because they depend on neither the split nor the probes:
- `dataset_steps_barplot.pdf` and `trajectory_tokens_histogram.pdf`, which come from the trajectory files.
- `label_prevalence_heatmap.pdf`, which comes from the label files.
- The trajectory UMAP figures. These are unsupervised and coloured by step, and use no probe weights.

`summary.json` holds the `_weighted_mean` check, the headline numbers (submitted vs rerun), the transfer reference check and every plotted value.
